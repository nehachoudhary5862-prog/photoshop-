A visual design case study covering branding, advertising, identity and promotional design work created in Adobe Photoshop, with focus on typography, composition, colour and hierarchy.

Adobe PhotoshopBrandingAdvertisingLogo DesignVisual Identity
01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

This project presents a collection of selected graphic design works including a coffee brand poster and business card, a jewellery advertising banner and a conceptual logo redesign. Each design explores a different visual communication problem while maintaining a clean and professional presentation.

02 — PLANNING

Planning & Objectives

GoalCreate portfolio-ready graphic design pieces across multiple branding and advertising formats.
AudienceBrands, recruiters and clients looking for visual design, promotional and branding skills.
Design FocusTypography, composition, colour balance, hierarchy and visual consistency.
ToolAdobe Photoshop for layout, image treatment, mockups and final presentation.
03 — REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS

Requirements

  • Create visually strong promotional designs
  • Maintain readable typography and clear hierarchy
  • Use suitable colour palettes for each design category
  • Balance imagery, text and negative space
  • Prepare presentation-ready outputs for portfolio use
04 — DESIGN

Concept & Visual Direction

The design phase focused on selecting suitable references, defining visual direction, testing typography and arranging elements to create strong compositions. Each piece was developed according to its own branding or advertising purpose while keeping the overall presentation professional and consistent.

05 — DEVELOPMENT

Design Execution

LayoutCreated balanced compositions using grids, alignment and spacing.
TypographySelected and arranged fonts to build visual hierarchy.
Image TreatmentAdjusted imagery, lighting, contrast and placement for polished outputs.
Brand ElementsApplied colours, logo concepts and promotional styling based on each concept.
06 — TESTING & REVIEW

Design Review

  • Checked readability and text hierarchy
  • Reviewed alignment and spacing
  • Checked colour contrast and visual balance
  • Reviewed image quality and composition
  • Made final refinements before portfolio presentation
07 — DELIVERY

Final Output

The final designs were prepared as portfolio-ready visual pieces and presented as a complete graphic design project demonstrating branding, advertising and Photoshop-based visual design skills.
